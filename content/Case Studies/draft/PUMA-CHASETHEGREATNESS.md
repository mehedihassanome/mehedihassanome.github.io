## PUMA Flagship Outlet Launch – 'chasethegreatness' Campaign (Chattogram)

**Project/Campaign Title:** PUMA Flagship Outlet Launch – #chasethegreatness Campaign

**Client:** PUMA Bangladesh

**My Role:** Strategic and Planning Executive

**The Story:**

PUMA aimed to make a significant splash in Chattogram, Bangladesh's second-largest city, with the launch of its new flagship outlet. Having already established a strong presence in Dhaka, the challenge was to go beyond a simple store opening. We needed to create an unprecedented buzz, drive immense foot traffic, and solidify PUMA's dominant presence among the city's already fanatic fanbase, ensuring the launch became a memorable, talkable event. The objective was to convert interest into measurable engagement and sales.

My strategy was centered on developing an **innovative, integrated digital and on-ground campaign** that deeply resonated with the target audience. I led a cross-functional team in conceptualizing and implementing the "#chasethegreatness" initiative. A key differentiator was the incorporation of **AI-driven customer segmentation** to ensure personalized and highly effective outreach, as we understood that young audiences' engagement with PUMA as a brand would be a key factor. We built hype through a series of **viral social media teaser posts**, designed to keep the audience on their toes with surprise after surprise. We strategically engaged world-class cricketers Liton Das and Shakib Al Hasan to announce the store's launch, amplifying our reach. To further connect with Gen Z, we tactically used **memes with popular audio voice actors** to create humorous brand connections. We even transformed a motor car into a **life-like PUMA sports shoe** to generate astounding visual impact. The grand finale was a **huge launching show with Shakib Al Hasan**, revealed to an ecstatic crowd. The entire plan focused on leveraging digital platforms to amplify the physical activation, driving both online engagement and offline visitation.

**Results:**

The audacious strategy led to truly astounding results, showcasing the power of innovation and integrated marketing:

- Achieved a **150% increase in opening day foot traffic** compared to previous store launches.
    
- Recorded a **35% conversion rate on the campaign microsite**.
    
- Drove a **275% increase in social media engagement**.
    
- Secured **85% positive sentiment** in post-launch customer surveys.
    
- Earned a **Gold Award at the Flame Award Asia 2024 for "Best Innovative Campaign of the Year"**.
    
- Received the **2024 Bangladesh Digital Innovation Award for "Digital Excellence"**.
