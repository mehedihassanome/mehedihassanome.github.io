## PUMA Flagship Store Launching Campaign (Dhanmondi)

- **Project/Campaign Title:** PUMA Flagship Store Launching
    
- **Client:** PUMA Bangladesh
    
- **My Role:** Strategic and Planning Executive. I was instrumental in designing and executing the integrated campaign, planning and developing the engagement microsite, creating detailed content for the website, and resolving technical issues. This project showcased my excellent planning and critical thinking ability.
    

The Story:

Following the considerable success of PUMA's first flagship store in Banani, the brand aimed to expand its reach and create significant buzz for its second flagship store in Dhanmondi 27. The core challenge was to provide a unique integrated offline and online hybrid experience specifically targeting the young generation, acknowledging their extensive use of smartphones and social media.

Our overarching strategy was to **capture the rhythmic beating energy and active lifestyle of Dhanmondi and PUMA** through an **interactive outdoor visual** combined with a **unique mobile-first experience**. This involved creating a **creative mural art concept named the “Fanatic Wall”** at Rabindra Shorobor, featuring both Dhanmondi and PUMA with a "Coming Soon!" message. A **QR Code on the Fanatic Wall** directed tech-savvy users to a **mobile-optimized microsite, `www.puma-bd.com`**. To incentivize engagement, users were prompted to **upload a selfie with the Fanatic Wall** on the microsite and share it on Facebook & Instagram with specific hashtags to receive a **personalized PUMA discount**. To build anticipation, **famous national cricketers** like Shakib Al Hasan and Mushfiqur Rahim shared social media teasers about the “Fanatic Wall” prior to the launch. The entire PUMA Dhanmondi Store was **completely wrapped** to create an additional layer of mystery. Communication and engagement were further driven through **Digital Platforms** (Facebook, Instagram), **PR coverage**, and various **brand communications**. The Dhanmondi area was extensively branded with **banners, LED panels, and sky trackers**. The campaign ran from September 21, 2021, to October 2, 2021.

The Results:

The campaign achieved astounding results, demonstrating its effectiveness in generating significant engagement and driving foot traffic, and was nominated in the Outdoor category:

- The campaign garnered **more than 200,000 footfalls at the event**.
    
- It achieved **300,000 social media reach**.
    
- **More than 10,000 individuals registered with their selfies** for discount coupons.
    
- Winning the "**commaward in 2022 in the best outdoor campaign category**".
