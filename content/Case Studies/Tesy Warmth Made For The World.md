## TESY Bulgaria: Orchestrating a Year of Brand Building & Sales Growth**

- **Project/Campaign Title:** TESY Bulgaria: 360-Degree Year-Long Marketing Strategy & Brand Building
    
- **Client:** TESY Bulgaria, a leading European heating appliances brand.
    
- **My Role:** Strategic and Planning Executive.
    

The Story:

Our mission was to develop a comprehensive and sustained marketing approach for TESY Bulgaria, a European heating appliance brand, specifically to increase sales for their BiLight Anniversary edition by leveraging their 30-year anniversary. The core challenge was to build strong brand presence, boost consumer awareness, and drive sales of their heating appliances across Bangladesh over an entire year, while also improving the association of TESY Electric Water Heaters (EWH) with "More value for the Money" and "SAVE POWER START WITH THE SHOWER". This required managing a complex, long-term brand initiative that deeply resonated with the local market.

I designed and executed a **full-fledged, 360-degree year-long marketing strategy** for TESY Bulgaria, focusing on a multi-channel integration. My approach involved developing **emotionally compelling communication materials and key visuals** tailored to the Bulgarian audience, aiming to foster strong brand connection and acceptance as a reliable partner. We focused on **target audience segmentation**, identifying distributors, traditional trade, end consumers, retailers, and SMEs, understanding their motivations and behaviors. Our core messaging emphasized TESY's **innovation, technology, and warmth**, positioning the brand as synonymous with Quality, Reliability, Comfort, Technology, Sustainability, Innovation, Durability, Performance, and Safety, including its Eco Smart Mode. We deployed content across **Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Google**, structuring content around Brand Value, Product Value, Offer Proposition, and Engagement. The campaign was strategically phased, with a **Summer phase** focusing on brand awareness and the 30 years of success, and an **Autumn phase** shifting to product offers and USPs. We utilized a variety of ad formats, including **Instant Experience Storytelling, Dynamic Creative Ads, Carousel Ads, Messenger Ads, YouTube in-video Ads, and GDN Static/GIF Ads** for optimal reach and engagement. Beyond client-facing efforts, I also implemented **modern task management applications** (like ClickUp and Trello) within the agency to foster better work improvement and cooperation, and mentored two junior team members.

The Results:

This strategic initiative demonstrated my capability in managing complex, sustained marketing projects, yielding significant results both for TESY and our agency:

- The campaign successfully gained **widespread traction** and significantly enhanced our agency's acceptability as a supportive partner in Bangladesh.
    
- My implementation of task management applications **improved team efficiency by 25%** within the agency.
    
- I achieved a **65% client acquisition rate** through successful pitches.
    
- The project showcased my analytical strengths in turning strategy into tangible results and effectively resonating with the target audience.
